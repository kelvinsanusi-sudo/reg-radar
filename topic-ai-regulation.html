Government trend & party positions, plus tracked documents in this area
The UK government has shifted from proposing binding regulation on large AI model developers toward a sector-based regulatory approach using existing regulators. Labour's 2024 manifesto pledged binding regulation on the most powerful AI model developers, but by February 2025 pivoted to regulating most AI systems at the point of use through existing sector regulators rather than a single AI law.
